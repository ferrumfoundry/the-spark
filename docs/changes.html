What's changing.

What's releasing, and what's not continuing — stated plainly, in one place.

Releasing · December 22, 2026

The Spark

Onyx, Rune and Talon — Ferrum's team of three AI teammates — launch in The Spark app on December 22, 2026. Subscriptions open the same day: the full team for $60 a month in The Spark app, or one Spark for $20 a month inside the Ironworks app. Until launch, nobody is charged for anything.

Retiring · Announced November 27, 2026

The original Echo models are retiring.

The original Echo models — the ones built for the hackathon app — are retiring, and will no longer be available. Ferrum is moving on from the Echo models, and moving over to the Sparks. Onyx, Rune and Talon are the team now, and nothing about The Spark changes.
