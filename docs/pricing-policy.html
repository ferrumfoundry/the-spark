Policy

Pricing & seats policy.

The plain rules for Spark prices, one-account subscriptions, and Spark Seats.

Subscription prices

One subscription, one account.

Regular Spark subscriptions are for one account only. The full team is $45 a month during the Black Friday sale (regularly $60), and one Spark is $15 a month during the sale (regularly $20). Sale prices hold until after Christmas. Subscriptions open December 22, 2026, and nobody is charged before launch.

A person cannot keep buying the same subscription again to stack more usage or more tokens onto the same account. Buying the same plan more than once does not add extra usage or tokens to that account.

Spark Seats policy

Spark Seats are different.

Spark Seats is its own subscription for businesses, and it is not combined with any other Spark subscription. Spark Seats are $100 a month during the Black Friday sale (regularly $200 a month).

Spark Seats are made for more than one person at a business. Each seat is for one person. A business uses Spark Seats when different people each need their own seat — instead of buying the regular one-account subscription over and over.

The difference

Accounts and seats are not the same.

A regular Spark subscription belongs to one account. Spark Seats belong to a business's own separate subscription, with a seat for each person. That is the difference, and it keeps the prices and usage fair for everyone.
