How it works

Three steps. That's the job.

From brief to done, this is all there is to it — no setup, no machine to keep running at home. The Sparks and their computer live on Ferrum's side.

  1. 1

    Brief the team

    Tell Onyx what you need, in chat or by phone. He takes the brief, plans the job, and the work starts on the computer.

  2. 2

    Watch the work

    The computer screen is live in the app, and the action record shows every step as it happens. Open the screen to watch — or take over. If it isn't in the record, it didn't happen.

  3. 3

    Approve, then it ships

    Anything that reaches the outside world — sending, posting, deleting, spending — waits on your approval card. Nothing risky moves without your tap. Then Onyx reports back.

Safety, built in

Helpful, and held to account.

A team that can do real work has to answer for it. Three layers do that job — plus one hard rule underneath them all.

A judge checks first

Every action is screened before it runs. Money, sending, posting, deleting and credentials always stop at the judge — no exceptions.

Approval cards

Anything risky waits for your tap — Do it, or Don't. Nothing moves on a guess, and the card says plainly what you're approving.

The record

Every action the team takes is written into a record you can reopen any time. If it isn't in the record, it didn't happen.

Contained, always

The Sparks run inside Ferrum's service and never leave it. No copies elsewhere, no wandering off — that's the rule they were built under.

Lessons that stick

Lessons from every job are kept and recalled, so the team gets better at your work instead of starting from zero each morning.

Its own phone line

Onyx has his own number and answers calls. Talk to the host like a person — the memory and the record come with him.

Before you ask

Frequently asked.

What is The Spark?

A team of three AI assistants — Onyx, Rune and Talon — who live in an app and work on their own real computer. You brief them in chat or by phone, watch them work on a live screen, and approve anything risky before it happens. Onyx hosts, Rune scouts, Talon builds.

When does it launch?

December 22, 2026. Subscriptions open at launch — until then, nobody is charged for anything.

What can they actually do?

Real work on a real computer: write and edit files and notes, open apps, work the mouse, gather and read, and answer Onyx's phone line. Every action is written into a record you can check. And they don't guess at the risky steps — those stop and ask you first.

Is it safe?

Three layers. A safety judge checks actions before they run — money, sending, posting, deleting and credentials always stop for your approval card. The action record shows everything that happened. And the Sparks are contained: they run inside Ferrum's service and never leave it.

What do I need?

A phone and the app. The Sparks and their computer run on Ferrum's side, so there's no machine to set up or keep on at home.

Who makes The Spark?

Ferrum, an independent AI company. The Spark — and Onyx, Rune and Talon themselves — were designed and built by Jakob, Ferrum's founder.
