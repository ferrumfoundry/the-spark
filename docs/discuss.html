House rules

Keep it kind

Be kind. Disagree with ideas, not with people. No harassment, no spam, and no posting anyone else's private information. Ferrum looks after this space, and anything that breaks these rules comes down — everyone is welcome here as long as it stays friendly.

Bugs & requests

Found a bug instead?

Bug reports and feature requests go in the repository's Issues, where they keep a trail Ferrum can work from. The discussion is for talking — Issues is for fixing.
